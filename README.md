# Customer Churn Analysis - Business KPI Dashboard
1. Project Overview
The Customer Churn Analysis project uses Microsoft Power Bl to analyse customer churn, retention, tenure, and revenue risk. The dashboard presents key business performance indicators and visualizations to help understand customer behaviour and identify areas where customer retention can be improved.
2. Objective
• Analyse customer churn and retention.
• Understand customer distribution across different tenure groups.
• Compare churn patterns across payment methods.
• Examine customer churn by contract type.
• Present important business KPls in an interactive dashboard.
3. Tools and Technologies
• Microsoft Power BI Desktop
• Power Query for data preparation, if used
• DAX for KPI calculations
• Customer churn sample dataset in CSV format
4. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
• Total Customers: 15
• Churn Rate: Approximately 46.67%
• Retention Rate: Approximately 53.33%
• Average Tenure: 18.80 months
• Revenue at Risk: 409.93, as calculated in the dashboard
5. Dashboard Visualizations
A. Churn by Tenure Band
A stacked column chart compares churn status across three customer tenure groups:
• 0-12 months
• 13-24 months
• 25+ months
This visualization helps compare churn patterns among newer and longer-tenured customers.
B. Churn by Payment Method
A horizontal bar chart compares the displayed churn-rate measure across payment methods, including Debit Card, UPI, and Credit Card.
C. Churn by Contract Type
A chart is intended to compare customer churn across contract categories, such as month-to-month, one-year, and two-year contracts.
D. Overall Analysis
A pie chart displays the measures included in the dashboard's overall analysis section.
6. Business Insights
• The displayed churn rate indicates that customer churn is an important area to investigate.
• The retention rate indicates that more than half of the customers in the sample are retained.
• The tenure-band visualization helps identify customer groups for further retention analysis.
• Comparing payment methods and contract categories can help identify segments that may need closer investigation.
7. Conclusion
     This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform customer data into KPI cards and visual reports. The dashboard provides a starting point for analysing customer retention and identifying opportunities to improve customer loyalty.
